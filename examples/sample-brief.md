# Sample Brand Brief

[Back to the README](../README.md)

This is an invented example. The Example Shop is not a real business. Copy the shape of the brief, not its content.

- Who it is for: office workers in their thirties who like dry humour and buy shirts to wear at the weekend.
- Tone: deadpan, warm, understated. It must never feel loud, motivational or like a corporate slogan.
- What you never do: no hustle slogans, no sports teams or brand names, no celebrity faces, no copying any named artist.
- Products: cotton T-shirts and vinyl stickers.
- Creative territories: office rituals, houseplants, small domestic routines.
