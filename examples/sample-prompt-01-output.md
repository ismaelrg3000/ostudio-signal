# Sample Output for Prompt 01

[Back to the README](../README.md)

This is an invented example of what Prompt 01 can return. The sign, the shop and the answer were made up for this page. A real model will answer differently, and you must check any real-world fact yourself.

## What Was Sent

The brand brief from [sample-brief.md](sample-brief.md), then the creative-director instruction from the README, then Prompt 01 with this reference:

> REFERENCE: A photo I took of a hand-painted rules sign beside a community swimming pool. Tall condensed capital letters read "NO RUNNING". A black pictogram of a figure mid-stride sits inside a red circle with a diagonal bar. The paint is chipped at the corners.

## What Came Back (Shortened)

1. **Literal content.** Documented (source: the description above): a sign, condensed capitals, a pictogram inside a prohibition circle, chipped paint.
2. **Context and unknowns.** Unknown: who painted it, when, and whether the lettering was copied from a template. Nothing else is claimed.
3. **Visual mechanisms.** Interpretation: tall letters read as an order, and the circle-and-bar format says "stop" before the words do. The chipped paint adds age and a human hand.
4. **Emotional and social signals.** Interpretation: mild authority, shared rules, the small public ritual of being told off.
5. **Conceptual mechanism.** Interpretation: role reversal. A rule aimed at everyone becomes a private joke when it protects something pleasant.
6. **What makes it distinctive.** Interpretation: it is plain and urgent at the same time.
7. **Principles to adapt.** A prohibition format can carry a gentle rule. Imperfect hand lettering signals care.
8. **Do not reproduce.** The exact lettering, the pictogram, and any logo that appears on a real sign.

## Three Original Directions

- **NO PLANS.** A small potted plant inside a prohibition circle, lettering drawn fresh. T-shirt. Invented meaning: a weekend with nothing booked.
- **QUIET HOUR.** A clock face with a diagonal bar across it, made as a sticker. The meaning is invented too: protected time between meetings.
- **NO REPLY ALL.** Office etiquette written as a rule sign, also a sticker. Its meaning, also invented: a small mercy for every inbox.

## Creative Brief

Audience: office workers who like dry humour. Effect: a smile of recognition. Tension: a rule sign that protects something pleasant. Territory: office rituals and small routines. Product: T-shirt first, sticker second. Success: readable at sticker size and funny without a caption.
