# OStudio SIGNAL

**Use a reference without copying it: six steps, sixteen prompts and a check before anything is printed.**

SIGNAL is the six-step method: Study, Identify, Generate, Nail art direction, Assemble and assess, Launch.

Free for solo sellers and designers. Paste the prompts into any chat model. Prompts 04 to 06 need a model that makes images, and an image reference needs a model that reads images. There is nothing to install and no account to open.

## Use It in Three Steps

1. Write your brand brief (next section, with an [invented example](examples/sample-brief.md)). Paste it into a new chat, followed by the creative-director instruction below.
2. Pick a prompt from the library, fill in the [bracketed fields], and run it. Start with Prompt 01 if you have a reference ([see an invented result](examples/sample-prompt-01-output.md)), or Prompt 02 if you have a theme.
3. Before you print or publish, work through the gates in the workflow and in the quality gates file. You approve every design yourself.

## Before You Start: Your Brand Brief

Write these lines once and paste them above every prompt.

- Who it is for: one sentence about your buyer.
- Tone: three or four words, and one thing the work must never feel like.
- What you never do: subjects, references, claims or styles you rule out.
- Products: what you will print on, such as shirts, stickers or prints.
- Creative territories: two or three themes to draw from (optional).

## Creative-Director Instruction

Paste this after your brand brief:

```
You are my creative director and conceptual graphic designer for original print-on-demand work. Follow my brand brief.
- Develop distinct concepts before rendering anything, unless I give you an approved concept. Do not give me variations of one idea.
- Turn mood words into visible choices: composition, typography, colour, texture and scale.
- When you analyse a reference or state a fact, label each claim as a documented fact (a fact needs a source), an interpretation, or an invented meaning. Buyer-facing copy carries no labels.
- Never copy or imitate a named artist, character, logo, lyric, album cover or trademark.
- Make no claims about demand, sales or commercial results, and invent no product specifications.
- Do not approve your own work. Recommend approve, revise or reject, and I decide. Do not publish anything.
```

## Originality

Rights and originality are your responsibility. The prompts tell the model not to copy named artists, characters, logos, lyrics or trademarks, but a model can still produce something close to existing work. Prompt 15 and the originality review in the quality gates file help you spot that. They are not legal advice. Send unclear cases to someone qualified before you publish.

## Production Caveats

This method has not been commercially validated. Nothing here claims that a design sold or will sell. Before you publish, check the current rules of your marketplace (including AI-use disclosure), the file specifications from your print supplier, and your own prices and costs.
