# OStudio SIGNAL: Production & Listing Quality Gates

[Back to the README](../README.md)

These gates sit behind Gates A and L of the [workflow](../docs/signal-workflow.md).

## Design approval

A design cannot move into production until:
- The concept has a clear reason to exist.
- The artwork is original enough to stand independently.
- The visual hierarchy works at intended size.
- Typography and wording have been checked.
- The design suits the product.
- The production method can reproduce important details.
- The asset has been reviewed for originality and rights risks.
- Human approval is recorded.

## Asset checks

- Confirm source file and export dimensions.
- Verify transparent background when required.
- Inspect edges, gaps, fine lines, and small text.
- Verify colour mode and output format against supplier instructions.
- Check the print area and physical dimensions.
- Test contrast on actual garment colours.
- Avoid relying on tiny details that disappear at thumbnail or print size.
- Keep an untouched master asset.

## Listing checks

- Product name and attributes are accurate.
- Images accurately represent the product.
- Available colours and sizes are verified.
- Material, fit, print method, and care claims are supported.
- Production and shipping estimates match current settings.
- Returns and policies match shop settings.
- Title and tags are relevant and not misleading.
- AI disclosure is included where required.
- Price and contribution margin have been calculated.
- Human approval is recorded before publication.

## Originality review

- Does the design reproduce a recognisable protected character, logo, lyric, album cover, or distinctive brand identifier?
- Could it be mistaken for official merchandise?
- Is it too close to a specific reference in composition or wording?
- Does it remain interesting if the reference is removed?
- Is a licence or permission required?

Do not provide legal guarantees. Escalate uncertain cases for qualified review.

## Listing-image plan

1. Primary product image.
2. Artwork close-up.
3. Placement and scale.
4. Lifestyle context.
5. Size/fit details if useful.
6. Accurate variant overview.
7. Design-story image only when it adds value.

Check current marketplace rules for acceptable mockups and real product imagery before publishing.

## Project record

Before a product is published, its entry in the project record must be complete: see the field list in the [workflow](../docs/signal-workflow.md#project-record). Do not publish while the originality checks, the human approval or the price and contribution margin fields are empty. After launch, add any performance evidence to the same entry.
